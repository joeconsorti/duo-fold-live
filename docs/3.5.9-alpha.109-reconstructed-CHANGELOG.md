# 3.5.9-alpha.109-reconstructed-local

Fix a false closing animation while the phone is physically flat. In the supplied alpha.108 report, session 108724393 ended at a reported 172°, then session 108726919 started at 171° roughly 220 ms after cleanup. Its angle remained 171° until the stationary-hinge return. The Android base state stayed OPENED. Receipt timestamps do not establish hardware sensor freshness or explain the near-flat angle offset.

## Change

- Extend the existing enabled-by-default jitter-protection toggle to both endpoints, retaining its stored preference key and the user's choice. Rename its visible label to “Hinge jitter protection”.
- Add open-end hysteresis before pause, animation, preview, rotation, and display-control decisions in the shared angle reader. At the default open endpoint of 172°, a latched open phone stays at an effective endpoint for reported 170–171°; closing is accepted immediately at 169° or below. The separation is three degrees relative to the user's configured open threshold.
- Latch on every fresh open-end reading and conservatively on reader initialization, reset, setting changes, or protection re-enabling. This also covers the first fresh reading arriving at 171° with no preceding 172° sample.
- After accepted closing movement, pass through the angle until the open endpoint is reached again. Genuine opening still reaches the original endpoint. No timer, sustained-speed condition, extra sensor subscription, capture, or power request is added.
- Preserve the existing closed-end gate and independent physical CLOSED veto. Disabling the same toggle restores the original angle behavior. Report open-end raw/effective values, boundaries, and suppressed excursions separately from the closed gate.
- Retain alpha.108's preview vignette and all controller/rendering code. VersionCode 1362; package org.duofold.live.test. Alpha.107 remains the stable rollback.

## Verification

The JVM suite covers stationary readings, repeated 171↔172° chatter, first-reading startup, immediate real closing, opening completion, stale/invalid input, threshold changes, toggling, resets, and coexistence with the closed gate and physical veto.

The deterministic controller replay runs the actual filters, HingePausePolicy and LiveB8Controller against the existing fake Android platform. With protection disabled, 171° reproduces the false start. With protection enabled, 60 seconds of 170–172° readings creates no session, capture scene or power request; 169° starts immediately; a real closing leg still prepares the cover; returning to 172° cleans up; further 171↔172° chatter cannot restart. It tests both a prior endpoint and a first sample below the endpoint.

Build and replay checks are software verification. On-device visual acceptance remains pending. Suggested handset check: leave the phone flat, including after unlocking; then perform normal slow and fast closes/opens and verify both existing animation directions.
