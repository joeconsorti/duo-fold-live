# Alpha.90 · Incoming app frost

Alpha.89 sent the app preview through a single texture lookup, while Home also supplied its glass painter with a seven-level blur pyramid. The app path now feeds that same filtering stage from a coarser, centered crop. It also applies the original Home waiting veil’s fixed, clamped blur (3.5% of the shorter side) and subtle white-to-black tint. The veil covers the entire incoming app panel even when hinge shading is zero. It follows the hinge while the app is waiting and dissolves over the accepted destination image.

The app frost layer records at up to 96 pixels on its long edge. It reuses the node, blur effect and source texture through the fold. The preview is eligible in Duo Mode even on a frame rendered without animation. A ready destination on the first frame skips the veil. Home, workspace, task routing, capture sizes, physical-panel checks and endpoint timing retain their existing behavior.
