# Alpha.88 · Full-panel app previews

While Duo waits for an app to draw on the incoming display, it now scales the outgoing screenshot uniformly until it covers that panel and crops the excess equally on opposite edges. The backdrop and animated glass share the same placement. The temporary inner preview covers both halves. During the temporary cover preview, missing glass samples use the same cropped backdrop.

This applies when a regular app’s captured physical panel differs from the receiving panel. The original capture keeps its source identity throughout the preview. The renderer switches to the destination layout when a matching capture arrives. Home and workspace use their existing presentation policies.

To check on the SM-F971U, start in a portrait app with recognizable content near the middle, such as the YouTube feed. Close slowly while watching the cover, then unfold while watching the inner panel. On each incoming display the preview should reach all four edges before the app adopts its new layout.
