# Alpha.99 — outgoing-only hinge hold

Includes alpha.98 per-panel Duo FPS reporting and alpha.97 rendering changes.

The pause policy previously selected cover/inner from the confirmation angle,
irrespective of the outgoing panel. Thus a long fold followed by a stationary
incoming-side interval could latch the destination and suppress further motion.
This behavior was present in the shared policy; the code does not establish that
alpha.97 introduced it. Repeated wallpaper values are not hardware sensor proof.

For Duo mode, retain the outgoing panel from the native endpoint (closed means
cover; open means inner), or the initial primary when enabled mid-fold. Early
primary remapping cannot replace that origin. A cover-origin opening is eligible
only at or below 90 degrees; an inner-origin closing only at or above 90 degrees.
Incoming-side time clears the pending timer and cannot accumulate toward a hold.
Crossing after a completed hold resumes animation without rearming on the incoming
side. Reaching the next endpoint establishes the next outgoing panel.

The 1.5-second stillness interval, 5-degree tolerance, 280 ms unwind and 160 ms
dissolve remain. Live Screen/legacy selection behavior is unchanged. Tests cover
both directions, remapping, slow continuous closing, crossing after a hold, full
timer restart and the next leg. Host tests cannot verify physical display pixels
or diagnose unseen sensor stalls. Delivery uses the clean committed build workflow.
