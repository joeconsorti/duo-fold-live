# 3.5.9-alpha.68-reconstructed — selectable system-bar experiments

Replaces alpha.67's stretched source-panel bar fallback. That fallback's mapping
policy and tests have been deleted; no experiment substitutes another physical
panel's bar images. Historical alpha.67 notes remain historical records.

Three mutually exclusive toggles appear under the resolution controls. All are
OFF initially, including upgrades from alpha.67. Selection is pinned for each
animation; changes take effect at the next animation. All off restores normal
Duo presentation without supplemental bars. Adaptive resolution and other saved
controls remain unchanged.

1. Native system bars: no bar pixels are captured. Stable, bounded, drawn bar
   window geometry on the current primary display determines a crop of the
   entire owned scene, including the black guard. Full-span edges only; missing,
   partial-width, fullscreen or stale windows do not create guessed holes.
   Geometry expires after 1 second, and primary identity, rotation, size and lock
   state are checked again at buffer submission. Crop and buffer are submitted
   together. Every physical layer-stack exchange atomically removes both crops;
   native exposure resumes only with fresh evidence. The effect is clipped, not
   resized. This does not create SystemUI on the secondary screen, reorder OEM
   surfaces, or establish visible-pixel readiness.
2. Destination-only snapshots: only current-session images matching the physical
   destination, full dimensions and rotation are drawn at their original bounds.
   Existing guarded captures may provide the pixels. Additional bounded captures
   retry independently of app-readiness callbacks once the destination is D0.
   Missing navigation/taskbar content remains missing; no source fallback.
3. Per-screen cached snapshots: adds a process-local cache of each screen's own
   primary bar capture. A fold/unfold cycle populates both screens when Android
   actually supplies the bars. Entries expire after 5 minutes; at most two panels
   and 8 MiB of cropped pixels are retained. No disk storage. Screen-off, service
   destruction, changing the method, or Clear cached system bars clears the cache.
   In-flight work cannot repopulate a cleared cache. Clock, notification and
   taskbar imagery can be stale and is not interactive. Fresh captures replace
   only their own edges; a status-only refresh preserves an unexpired bottom
   image from the same physical screen. Each cached edge keeps its original age.

All optional requests share a single global in-flight gate, including across
animation sessions. Snapshot requests have at most 12 attempts per physical
primary with 250 ms spacing after completion; native geometry has at most 60
attempts with 400 ms spacing, bounded by the existing 30-second scene lifetime.
Both success/partial results and failures observe the cooldown. Capture replies
are rejected after closure or mapping/geometry/lock changes. No new wake locks,
SystemUI policy mutation, app capture replacement, or readiness dependency.
The experiments are supported on the independent-layer Android 15+ route only.

## Evidence and limitations

The alpha.67 handset report showed source navigation fallback on inner and an
inner-native status capture only after the open endpoint; the first attempt was
rejected because another window dump was active. This supports removing that
fallback, but does not establish that any new experiment will show an inner
Samsung taskbar before native remapping. Fullscreen taskbar windows remain
ineligible rather than exposing/copying unrelated app content. Device testing
is required; no optical result is claimed by unit tests or transaction callbacks.

## Validation

- Debug unit tests cover exact panel/size/rotation matching, crop bounds, stale
  and future timestamps, cache eviction/expiry/clearing and bounded retries.
- Fake-compositor scene tests verify both native crops are removed atomically
  at a mapping exchange, alongside existing guard cleanup and lease checks.
- Run the standard committed-source local build and lint before delivery.

## Handset comparison

Select one toggle, then fold and unfold with the same app and speed. For cached
mode repeat the cycle once to populate both panels. Record whether status,
navigation and inner taskbar are visible, distorted, stale or delayed. The report
identifies OFF/NATIVE_EXPOSURE/DESTINATION_ONLY/PER_SCREEN_CACHE, capture outcomes,
cache age or missing images, and the native content crop. All toggles off is the
baseline. Native geometry and image commits are software evidence, not proof of
what either panel displayed. No recording is required for this comparison.
