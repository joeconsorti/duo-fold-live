# 3.0.4-alpha.22

- Removes the glass appearance preview and its slider from Fine-tune your animation.
- Adds enabled-by-default texture anti-aliasing to both projected and Classic glass shaders, covering live and screenshot paths independently of blur amount and glass strength. Uses the existing bilinear mipmaps with a small minimum trilinear filtering level, without extra full-screen passes or sample loops. Native mirrored content and the debug black gradient are not recaptured or postprocessed.
- Advanced contains an off toggle and a tightly bounded 20–50% filter adjustment (35% default/reset). This is subtle texture smoothing, not reconstruction of missing capture detail.
- Retains manual frame-rate selection and the experimental 120 FPS default.

Build checks cannot establish perceptual quality: verify text clarity, shimmer and performance on-device before promoting this candidate to the primary release.
