# Alpha.86 · YouTube fold handoff

The alpha.85 YouTube trace on the SM-F971U showed the cover going from upright to sideways and back for roughly 1.1 seconds. Duo had requested the cover at 158° while its rotation worker was still saving the user's settings. Both physical displays also reported OFF near three primary changes. One separate unfold fell back when a fresh brightness sample was unavailable. The software observations do not identify which unfold had the bright taskbar and dim center.

## Changes

- Duo checks both physical panels against the saved orientation for that panel and the current fold session. Before moving a fullscreen app, it requires a fresh paired rotation readback, a valid brightness sample on both screens, and the committed outgoing image. It rechecks rotation after starting the brightness hold and before requesting the primary change.
- A missing rotation readback has a two-second budget once the transfer reaches that check. Android takes over if the check expires or the fold reaches its endpoint first. If the fold ends while Duo is still saving rotation preferences, Duo discards the unused journal before freezing either screen. The saved handoff angle remains independent of the readiness trigger on this route.
- The physical renderer retains its last submitted buffer if its screen changes size or rotates. It checks again when the GPU finishes. A same-panel screenshot with incompatible geometry cannot be drawn into the old raster. Projection of the source screenshot onto the other panel is still available during handoff.
- Exact app captures record mean alpha and image luma for upper, middle and lower interior samples before Duo adds glass or system bars. An opaque black video frame is still eligible. The exporter skips missing rotation-status fields between detailed polls so empty events do not displace useful fold history.

## Handset check

Install over alpha.85 to keep the comparison settings. Use the same video and orientation as before and repeat one open and close. If the bright taskbar and dim middle recur, save the fold-image diagnostic ZIP immediately after that unfold. A camera recording from another device can show whether the physical display went dark while Android reported an ON screen.

This reconstructed build was prepared for handset testing. The timeout, retained buffers, display power readings and app capture samples cannot establish on-screen brightness or blackout duration.
