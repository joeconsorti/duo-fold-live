# 3.5.0 — Main release

Promotes the user-tested alpha.29 feature set to the official release.

- Lightweight Texture Filtering is the default anti-aliasing method. Edge-Adaptive Smoothing and 4× Supersampling remain selectable experiments in Advanced settings; existing selections are preserved.
- Frosted Reflection is the default left preview, with Animated Reflection available in Advanced settings. The right cover preview is committed first.
- Includes the device-independent animation alignment fix, adjustable blur and glass strength, center-edge blending, and manual 60/120 FPS content targets (120 FPS experimental by default).
- Includes persistent custom wallpaper recovery and support reminder cadence: first after three days, then 7, 14 and 30 days apart, then every 30 days, respecting snooze and dismissal.

Known issues remain: Samsung's display-switch blackout, occasional custom wallpaper flashes on unlock, unreliable Keep Awake, and occasional transition glitches. Earlier rollback downloads remain available; alpha.24 remains marked BROKEN.
