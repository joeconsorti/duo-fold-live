# Alpha.95 — release stuck frost and reduce idle work

Version 3.5.9-alpha.95-reconstructed-local, code 1348. Based on committed alpha.94.

The supplied alpha.94 Plex session (883276708) reached its opening endpoint at
883278842. A destination app capture succeeded at 883280126 and HAS_DRAWN at
883280245, but the inner effect retained its original 1848x2448 / rotation 3
geometry after the native panel returned to 2448x1848 / rotation 0. Its native
texture count stayed zero. Readiness publication depended on that renderer's
commit, and the controller required another effect commit to uncover the native
app. The overlay survived until the phase-5 timeout at 883283446, roughly 4.6 s
after the endpoint. Alpha.94's post-cleanup endpoint check could not release it.

Changes:

- Independent renderers follow stable powered physical-panel orientation after
  75 ms, rejecting the other panel's transient geometry. Resizing reuses the same
  root and pixel layer; old pixels stay installed until replacement pixels and
  scale submit together. All orientations share the original three-buffer cap.
  Unreleased buffers are neither overwritten nor reused for the new raster.
- Direct native return and Android-cancellation recovery check native policy,
  physical panel, power, selected task layout and two fresh native-window
  observations. They no longer require a screenshot or commit from the effect
  being removed. The normal moving handoff keeps its capture/commit gates.
- Native return stops new captures, effect redraws and concurrent-panel power
  renewals. It has an 1800 ms recovery budget; failure still cleans up owned
  coverage and logs a timeout rather than claiming native readiness.
- Mapping uncertainty no longer restarts the same destination capture's short
  retry burst. Obsolete deliveries are discarded.
- A settled endpoint with no scene polls every 100 ms after 750 ms, versus 32 ms
  before. Active scenes and observed travel use 16 ms. This reduces idle polling
  from about 31 to 10 requests/s; detecting a new fold can take up to one 100 ms
  idle interval. Urgent completion notifications still bypass the cadence.
- Wake preparation requires four degrees of closing travel, preventing the
  reported 178–180 degree wobble from binding a helper and taking a wake lock.
- Compatible upgrades retain saved controls; no settings reset is needed.

Validation covers the real controller against fake Android state (both directions,
missing texture/commit, wrong-panel and missing readiness, Android cancellation,
bounded cleanup), the compiled buffer renderer against fake GPU/compositor
callbacks, and orientation/polling/wake unit regressions. Final build and artifact
checks are performed using tools/build_local_test.py on the committed tree.

These tests do not measure physical display pixels, actual handset heat or input
latency. The log does not establish that the user's tap caused recovery; the
recorded route ends through its timeout. Heat has several possible contributors;
the changes remove demonstrated unnecessary app work, not a measured temperature
reduction. Test slow/fast Plex unfolding and closing in portrait and landscape,
then leave the phone open and still to check idle polling and thermal behavior.
