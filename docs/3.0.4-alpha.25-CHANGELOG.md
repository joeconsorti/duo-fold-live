# 3.0.4-alpha.25

- Fixes alpha.24's reflected-preview lifecycle feedback loop: left-view geometry now comes from the display rather than the transient shared frame. The reflected view remains mounted while capture frames arrive or clear.
- Creating/resizing the reflected consumer no longer clears the primary capture stream. This avoids repeatedly recreating surfaces, excluding them and rebuilding the compositor mirror.
- Closing wake hold renews with motion (five-second inactivity limit, thirty-second overall limit), rather than expiring five seconds after the first movement. Detects accumulated closing movement and reversal. A completed close releases shortly afterward.
- The report now keeps the wake guard outcome separately from the bounded lifecycle log. Fold-only recovery and manual power-button exclusions remain.
- Alpha.24 is marked BROKEN on GitHub; its original APK and download URL remain available.

The uploaded report confirmed repeated exclusion/mirror setup churn, but did not retain a wake-recovery result. The new report field is needed to verify Samsung's outcome; this build does not claim Keep Awake is proven fixed on the device.
