## Changes

1. Remove B8PanelGeometry's per-logical-display live-result cache and its
   invalidation listener. Identity, physical mode, and rotation can remain
   unchanged while measured bounds finish updating. Every lookup now reads
   current getRealSize; ON readiness never substitutes a saved size. Stored
   geometry remains a prewarm hint when a known panel is not ON. Unknown or
   invalid observations cannot overwrite saved dimensions. Matching values
   are not repeatedly written to preferences.
2. Report plain-primary coverage explicitly in UNMASK_WAIT, using the existing
   throttle. This changes diagnostics only: early animated release still needs
   its original native image, physical mapping, freshness, and coverage guards.
3. Preserve each known physical panel's animation-start result with its fold
   health row (existing 20-session bound). Skips now identify deadline, travel,
   endpoint, or invalid input. The 500 ms / 30 degree startup policy, its latch,
   and asymmetric starts are unchanged. This lets later fast folds be compared
   even after their detailed lifecycle records have rolled out of the log.

No settings are reset. Raster reuse, selected-task capture protections, two
post-remap native readiness observations, endpoint/cancellation coverage,
power cleanup, and the independent late-animation skip policy remain intact.
Live bounds are still software observations; the fix does not make independent
Android callbacks atomic or eliminate underlying compositor/layout delays.
