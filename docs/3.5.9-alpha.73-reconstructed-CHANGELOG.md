## Evidence and regression

The alpha.72 report shows Home using launcher UID 10162 and registered wallpaper
UID 10152. Its body was reconstructed from those two owner-filtered images, while
status/task bars came from a separate composed-display capture. The user reports
that the body now shows the underlying injected live wallpaper, but the bars show
the desired replacement photo. Alpha.72's background selection was too narrow:
a registered wallpaper UID does not include replacement photo surfaces owned by
another process. AOSP CaptureArgs.setUid explicitly filters by layer ownership.
The source also has below-app replacement-photo surfaces separate from the native
wallpaper window. The failure mechanism matches the body/bar split; the report
alone cannot identify which replacement surface was visibly active on this test.

The custom-wallpaper flag and host status in this report are false. Those are
local feature/process observations, not a compositor-wide inventory. They do not
prove which image was visible. This fix does not enable that flag or load a
retained disabled photo. It captures the existing composition with Duo effect
surfaces excluded, regardless of who owns its background photo.

The alpha.72 fade check rejected three destination samples with solid=0 before
accepting clear launcher samples. Preserve that check. The Home close at
404072624 also completed its power tail after Android cancellation, with the
cover remaining ON in the supplied callbacks and no wake attempt. Preserve
alpha.72's optional Home brightness and bounded endpoint power behavior.
The older ChatGPT/camera crashes do not establish a Duo crash in these sessions.

## Change

Native Home operation 9 still submits two captures with identical geometry and
Duo effect exclusions, using the existing shared 250 ms callback budget:

- A launcher-owner capture is used only for alpha/content validation.
- A composed-display Home capture supplies the actual image. It has no wallpaper
  owner filter and is drawn once, preserving the captured native layer order.

The launcher probe is never painted over the composed scene. This avoids doubling
icons, shadows and antialiased edges. The existing before/after Home focus, task,
window, wallpaper identity, physical display and geometry guards remain. Secure
results, errors and missing/late callbacks still reject and clean up their buffers.
Both captures remain required; there is no unvalidated fallback after a timeout.

A 64x64 central-body comparison also rejects substantial overpainting of otherwise
opaque launcher content. It compares probe alpha >=250 pixels with the scene,
allowing channel differences up to 32 and a changed minority (at most 25%) for
small inter-capture widget updates. At least 16 such pixels are required for this
comparison; sparse evidence is labelled. At least 95% of sampled scene-body pixels
must be opaque. Alpha fade/depletion checks run first. These are conservative
rejection heuristics, not proof of every icon or synchronization to one frame.
Rapidly changing large widgets or composition effects may cause extra retries;
existing session bounds and native fallback remain in control.

Diagnostics now say "composed Home retained once, wallpaper UID filter=off" and
"launcher probe validation only", with launcherAgreement mismatched/total and
existing pairMs/guardedTotalMs. Registered wallpaper owner remains a stability
observation, not a claim about the owner of the photo shown in the final image.

The independent bar capture, ordinary-app owner filtering, custom-wallpaper Home
window route, workspace route, glass settings/shader, adaptive resolution and
power/brightness controller are unchanged. No new capture timer or request is
added. One filtered background capture becomes a composed capture and one small
CPU comparison is added; real-device timing must still be measured.

paired-capture harness: 147 checks for owner selection, different-owner replacement
photo, geometry/exclusions, secure results, errors, both timeout directions and

old background UID restriction would return blue. Tests also cover single-draw

lint and existing bar/Home-window harnesses are checked before delivery.
