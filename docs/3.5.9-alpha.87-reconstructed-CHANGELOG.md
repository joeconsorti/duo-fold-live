# Alpha.87 · Faded app captures during a fold

The alpha.86 YouTube retest on the SM-F971U contains two destination captures that Duo accepted with very low opacity across the app. On the close starting at 601623433 ms the cover's upper, middle and lower bands all averaged 15 out of 255. On the unfold starting at 601626051 ms the inner screen's three bands all averaged 49. The outgoing app averaged 255 in every band in both sessions. The system-bar capture ran separately. These two sessions followed the six armed image sequences, so there is no matching compositor image of the reported dim middle.

## Cover rendering

During the first armed close the cover showed the projected YouTube image, then returned four near-black compositor samples at hinge readings of 127°, 123°, 112° and 100°. The saved native cover thumbnail for those samples was fully opaque and contained the feed. A desktop replay of the alpha.86 glass shader using that thumbnail and the recorded committed poses reproduced the near-zero image brightness in all four cases. Its projection sampled beyond the hinge edge of the texture and gave those samples zero weight against a black background.

For a regular app arriving on the cover, alpha.87 fills missing hinge-edge samples using the matching native cover image and applies the usual shading. The new shader kept the feed visible in all four replays. Ten other fully opaque destination images in the test sequence passed the separate capture check below.

## App capture

When the same fold has an opaque app reference, Duo rejects a new app capture if all three interior bands average less than 192 out of 255 in alpha. An opaque reference requires at least 240 in every band. These conservative thresholds distinguish the observed fade from dark video and from a single transparent strip. The decision uses the existing pixel grid before the bitmap is delivered or combined with system bars.

Duo keeps the previous image and requests a fresh capture on the verified panel. It records APP_CAPTURE_FADED with the band measurements. A rejected image cannot authorize destination readiness, even if the dark-frame retry budget has expired. Existing bounded recovery returns control to Android if a valid image never arrives.

## What the image sequences showed

All 125 recorded compositor images in the six armed folds reported rotation 0. Six images in five sequences were captured with the other panel's dimensions while the logical displays were swapping. The recorder now marks a mismatch against that physical renderer's dimensions as unavailable before counting a dark frame. A genuine quarter turn is accepted.

The first recorded close contains four consecutive near-black cover samples while the corresponding YouTube source thumbnail is opaque and contains visible content. Separately, 11 of the 12 YouTube primary requests from the first armed fold onward had a paired OFF observation 70–192 ms after the request. That is the time to a software observation. The data cannot establish blackout duration or what was visible between captures. Alpha.87 addresses the software projection and faded app snapshots. The physical power transition remains open.
