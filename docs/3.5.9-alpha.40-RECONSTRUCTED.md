## Changes

- Native fallback now has the same bounded 1800 ms coverage budget as Android cancellation. At an endpoint it cannot accept the opposite physical panel, even when base/current device states already agree. It never reasserts concurrent power after cancellation or brightness failure. Brightness freshness/restore safeguards are unchanged.
- Both normal reveal and cancellation release require two distinct successful app readiness observations spanning at least 100 ms, with session/check/physical/freshness guards. Reusing one HAS_DRAWN acknowledgement cannot satisfy the span. Failed readiness or geometry resets it. Existing deadlines remain bounded.
- Task geometry verification rejects large inconsistencies between max bounds and effective density/dp layout, before destination preparation and primary reveal. It tolerates small decor/rounding differences (96 px or 10% per dimension). No density, font scale or system zoom settings are written. This guards stale opposite-panel layout, not proof of pixel-perfect app sizing. Reveal density is logged again.
- Separate `Test animation + glass` switch in Screenshot mode, shown for coordinated screenshots, default false. Main Enable animation is still required. Effects choice is latched at session start; selected shader, blur, intensity and smoothing settings render on opaque retained screenshot coverage. Legacy remains plain. No live mirrors or continuous capture enabled by the switch.
- Plain coordinated screenshots skip seven blur pyramid levels per exact capture and defer FrostSurface/shader construction entirely. Animated screenshot submissions stop once visual angle converges; physical/layout/image changes and primary freshness checks still trigger buffers. Mid-fold 65/105 degree hold band is unchanged.
- Composition diagnostics avoid primary probes after the task has moved away. Glass mode pauses their fold budget and comparisons; automatic wallpaper pixel probes stay disabled. Source captures returned after a canceled session are recycled only when never published.

## Validation / limitations

140 fake-platform controller checks cover delayed physical remap after brightness failure, two-observation readiness and interruption, existing cancellation/cleanup/hinge policies; bridge cleanup and brightness harnesses pass. JUnit covers stale inner/cover dp layouts, alternate density, rotation, insets and unknown configuration. Delivery build runs full JUnit and APK assembly via tools/build_local_test.py.

No handset/ADB in this environment. Shader/device behavior, visible flashes, thermal improvement and final app scale require handset validation. Retained coverage still has a bounded timeout; this does not promise zero flashes in every system transition.
