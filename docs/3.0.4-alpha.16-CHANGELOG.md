# 3.0.4-alpha.16 — One full second of temporary wallpaper after unlock

User-directed timing experiment on the restored alpha.13/alpha.15 baseline. Keep the temporary custom photo for at least 1000 ms after its visibility submission and after USER_PRESENT, whichever is later. Home attachment readiness records readiness but cannot remove the temporary photo early. When Home is ready during the hold, move the temporary photo’s relative ordering to the Home anchor so lock-screen disappearance cannot end its hold. At the hold boundary, use the baseline atomic Home-placement/bridge-hide handoff. If Home is unavailable, remove the temporary photo with an explicit diagnostic instead of leaving it stuck.

The timer runs independently of the short wake polling window; delayed show or unlock notification extends it to preserve the full second. Repeated callbacks cannot extend it indefinitely. Sleep, shutdown, attachment failure and detection of another active app retain cleanup behavior. The photo is the same selected-image buffer. This does not claim measured physical panel visibility: SystemUI can still hide the relative parent, and phone testing is required.

No alpha.14 continuous polling or commit/next-frame handoff. No animation timing changes. The deliberate temporary overlay may remain above Home content during this test second; the user intends to reduce this duration after verifying reliability.

Validation: 15 focused selection, attachment and hold-policy tests, including early Home readiness, delayed show, unlock notification, repeat callbacks and sleep reset. Full release tests, lint, assembly and signing run in CI. Preserve alpha.13 as the known partly working rollback baseline.
