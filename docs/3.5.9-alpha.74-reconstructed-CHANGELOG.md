# Alpha.74 reconstructed local — endpoint response and unnecessary work

VersionCode 1327. Based on alpha.73 commit
ac0e3abb2202063518e92f83438bb47b8c107680. Recovery target remains alpha.20.
This is a reconstructed test build. The user confirms alpha.73 Home appearance
works; alpha.74 has host validation, not handset or optical verification.

## Report findings

The four latest Home motions in the supplied alpha.73 report have their first
two-panel presentation at 272–341 ms and PRIMARY_APP_READY at 1484–1660 ms.
Window guards reject unreported draw state and the launcher alpha probe rejects
solid=0 while Samsung Home fades in. These are useful rejections, not evidence
that the rejected frames were displayed. A valid capture costs roughly 204–320 ms
in the retained sessions, including before/after metadata; its pixel pair costs
50–126 ms. Readiness then requires distinct successful observations over 100 ms.
Do not remove these checks or replace them with a guessed sleep to shorten the
trace. The native Home capture and readiness scheduling remain unchanged here.

Frame gaps reach 318 ms in one retained close; other recent peaks are 69–129 ms.
At an opening endpoint, raw=176 can coexist with committedAngle around 149–151.
The independent renderer discards elapsed time above 50 ms on each draw and uses
120 ms smoothing all the way to immediate endpoint removal. That can increase
the pose discontinuity at native reveal. No log proves that a flash was visible.

Rotation restoration lasts about 1.2 seconds and sometimes overlaps the next fold.
The source restores and reads back both fixed policies, then unconditionally
restores and reads them again before verification. It also writes user_rotation
even if unchanged, and waits before its first verification query. The frozen=true
observations alone do not show an error: the saved user preference may be locked.

A fresh wake-helper connection and keep-awake transaction begin after the final
opening, with no sleep/wake event. The source arms this work on a one-degree drop
from the hinge peak, so full-open settling can trigger it. Existing endpoint
power cleanup is otherwise behaving as intended in these callbacks: coverage
removal is submitted in 1 ms, and the close power tail releases after Android
cancellation plus the existing 100 ms native-cover stability interval.

One accepted Home sample has solid=1204 but launcherAgreement=0/0. The fade guard
uses alpha >=240; agreement used >=250. Thus nearly opaque content could pass
without any scene-agreement anchors. This does not prove a bad displayed image.

Thermal status is 2, session raster is 25% per dimension, and app CPU is
26.3–29.5% of one core. These do not measure shell, compositor or GPU cost and do
not establish causation for thermal status. Historical ChatGPT/camera crashes
are not Duo crashes in these motions. Bar composition takes 3–8 ms once per
destination; it is not a demonstrated recurring bottleneck.

## Changes

- Independent-buffer smoothing consumes actual elapsed draw time up to 250 ms,
  once, retaining the newest hinge sample and never predicting future angles.
  A smooth blend removes remaining lag while approaching the final 40 degrees
  of opening or 16 degrees of closing. Mid-fold smoothness settings and the
  legacy renderer retain their behavior. Reversals remain bounded between the
  previous pose and current input. No extra endpoint frame, hold or wait is added.
  A sensor jump straight to an endpoint can still bypass a final rendered pose.
- Rotation release performs the fixed-policy restore once, verifies immediately,
  then requires another successful readback after at least 100 ms. A failed
  readback resets the streak and invokes the existing repair, bounded to ten
  observations. Failure retains the journal. An already matching user_rotation
  value is read but not rewritten. Panel/posture restoration order, physical
  identity checks, durable journal, recovery helper and ownership lease remain.
- Wake preparation requires three degrees of cumulative closing travel from the
  peak. One- and two-degree settling no longer bind helpers. Slow closure still
  accumulates travel; fast closure still arms immediately. Existing wake reason,
  expiry, lock, consent, renewal and 30-second bounds are preserved.
- Home agreement now includes the same alpha >=240 pixels accepted as solid by
  the fade guard. Their possible wallpaper contribution is at most 15/255,
  within the existing 32-channel comparison tolerance. Scene opacity, mismatch
  allowance, fade/depletion rejection, owner/window identity checks and the
  composed wallpaper image remain unchanged. Legitimate blank Home pages still
  report sparse evidence; probe pixels are never painted into the image.
- Optional brightness diagnostics distinguish pending preparation from a failed
  preparation instead of reporting "unavailable: null". Bar evidence describes
  its independent capture accurately for both app and composed-Home images.

lifecycle/readiness checks; brightness, scene, task, power and delayed-cleanup
