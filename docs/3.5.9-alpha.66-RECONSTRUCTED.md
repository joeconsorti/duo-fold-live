# 3.5.9-alpha.66 reconstructed — adaptive effect resolution

Default-on adaptive resolution skips directly from the selected maximum (default 100%) to 25% at Android thermal status 1 or higher. When status returns to 0, it permits 50%. After ten uninterrupted minutes at 0 it permits the selected maximum again. Any renewed pressure resets recovery. This uses thermal status, not battery-temperature thresholds.

The toggle sits below the resolution slider. The slider is always the upper bound; turning adaptation off restores that selection at the next animation. Resolution is pinned for each coordinated scene across both panels, source preparation, primary remaps and endpoint cleanup. Existing system thermal safety behavior is unchanged.

New/reset defaults are 100% resolution, 50% glass strength and 75% blur. Existing saved selections are retained; a previously saved 25% test setting can be cleared with Reset resolution.

Callbacks observe heating even outside animations. The recovery clock uses elapsed realtime, including sleep, without acquiring a wake lock. Thermal status is checked again at timer expiry and animation start. A process restart with pending recovery restarts the cool-down conservatively; toggling or restarting the service cannot erase the running thermal history.

Copy Report includes selected and next-animation resolution, thermal status/cap, cooldown time, session raster size, and effective resolution in each fold-health row. The optional 4x supersampling setting continues to double the chosen raster dimensions.

Validation: unit coverage exercises thermal escalation, 2-to-1 cooling, repeated cool readings, the exact ten-minute boundary, reheating, toggle bypass, slider maxima, failed readback, process-restart recovery and actual raster dimensions.
