# 3.5.9-alpha.59 reconstructed local — stationary hinge selection and jitter

Version code 1312. Private test APK based on alpha.58. Recovery target remains
alpha.20; reconstructed source does not establish equivalence with the original.
No default-mode change, settings migration, GitHub push or public release.

Only the deliberate stationary-hinge behavior changes:

- At hold confirmation, below 90 degrees selects the usable cover display and
  above 90 degrees selects the usable inner display, regardless of the starting
  panel or direction. At exactly 90 degrees the active panel is retained.
  The selected panel is latched for the hold; jitter across 90 cannot switch it.
- The stationary observation lasts 1250 ms of fresh sensor time. Excursions up to
  and including 5 degrees from its fixed observation anchor do not restart the
  timer; larger cumulative travel does. Once held, movement below 5 degrees from
  the held angle is ignored, and movement of 5 degrees or more in either direction
  resumes the animation. The anchor does not follow individual jitter samples.

The shared gesture feeds Live Screen and Screenshot modes. The coordinated
screenshot controller now honors that selected panel instead of overriding it
with the session's starting panel. Its task, readiness, dissolve, parking and
cleanup machinery remains unchanged. The 280 ms unwind and 160 ms screenshot
dissolve, saved glass/blur/stretch, normal moving handoffs, Home preparation,
endpoint release and fast-fold eligibility remain unchanged from alpha.58.

Validation covers the 1250 ms boundary, both sides of 90 and the exact tie,
opening/closing from either panel in every gesture route, jitter through the
observation interval, held jitter crossing 90, cumulative movement, exact 5-degree
resumption and re-holding on the other panel. Existing stale/duplicate sensor,
endpoint, mode, lock/disable and easing checks are retained. Production-controller
replays cover both selected panels from either origin, Home/apps and preparation/
post-handoff, plus all existing readiness, resume and cleanup checks.

No handset is connected. Automated checks are software evidence, not optical
proof. Device acceptance should confirm both modes settle to cover below 90 and
inner above 90, tolerate a shaky hold for 1.25 seconds, and resume on 5 degrees of
deliberate movement while preserving the alpha.58 transition appearance.
