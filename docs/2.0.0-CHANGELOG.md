# Duo Fold Live 2.0.0-alpha.1

**Pre-release for review. Not the official 2.0.0 release.**

## Animation styles

- **Windowed Glass (default):** the approved 1.9.0-alpha.2 animation, renamed. Inspired by iPhone Duo with even more windowed glass. The glass shader, handoff angles, black-fade policies, duration defaults, and closing-mirror reveal remain intact.
- **Duo Classic:** native inner-screen animation without the cover-screen mirror or the frosted left preview. Keeps the black handoff reveal.
- **Fold-Only:** Windowed Glass while closing; normal system behavior while opening.
- **Unfold-Only:** Windowed Glass while opening; normal system behavior while closing.
- The older Classic Glass shader and debug black shading remain under Advanced → Legacy & experimental visuals.
- Style selection and existing fine-tuning preferences persist across compatible updates. Direction-restricted modes use a small movement threshold to reject hinge jitter.

## Smoother transitions since 1.7.0

- Black fades before the physical display switch, followed by a controlled reveal on the destination display.
- Destination reveal waits for stable display ON and a matching software draw signal, with bounded fallback.
- The inner display's closing cover mirror has its own readiness/reveal clock, preventing a delayed mirror from missing its fade-in.
- Separate black-fade smoothing and gradualness controls; gentler defaults, with the original sharp fade available at 0% gradualness.
- Timers restart if a display turns OFF during reveal. Angle-driven fades can reverse as the hinge changes direction.
- The native full inner layout remains the normal experience. Experimental continuity testing has not solved Samsung's primary-display blackout without other compromises.

## Responsiveness, fluidity, and frame pacing

- Fade updates follow display frame callbacks, with a low-frequency fallback while display callbacks pause.
- Duplicate compositor transactions are skipped when opacity, crop, and display stack are unchanged.
- Cached display reflection, less frequent diagnostic formatting, and fewer redundant secondary-display refreshes reduce background work.
- Completion-paced angle polling now targets 4 ms, with immediate urgent handoff checks and no overlapping polls. This reduces polling overhead; it is **not** a claim that every angle round trip is faster than 1.7.0's 1 ms target.
- Capture/recovery and preview preparation have been refined across the alpha series to improve readiness and reduce visible disruption.
- Half-resolution glass by default, adjustable glass smoothing, and refresh requests up to 120 Hz are **retained from 1.7.0**, not newly introduced here.
- Render-submission rates are diagnostics, not measured displayed FPS. No guaranteed 120 FPS or quantified latency improvement is claimed without handset measurements.

## Keep-awake, wallpaper, and recovery

- Keep cover awake on close is an always-on saved app policy, verified in the background and reapplied when needed.
- Rechecks on screen events, setting changes, Shizuku availability, and permission restoration; pending rechecks are retained during an in-flight verification.
- Background supervision remains active independently of the settings screen. The stop action stops animation/wallpaper while keep-awake supervision remains.
- Custom wallpaper selection and enabled state persist across compatible updates and are restored after package replacement/reconnection when access is available.
- Boot recovery attempts resume saved work. Non-root Shizuku startup and TCP setup may still require user action after reboot.
- Improved display, connection, rendering, and recovery reports; version labels use the actual build version.

## App experience in this alpha

- Corrected title: **Duo Fold Live**.
- Restyled settings with rounded grouped cards, restrained blue accents, larger touch targets for style selection, and clear descriptions.
- Order: animation switch → styles → troubleshooting → fine-tuning → wallpaper → connection/setup → advanced diagnostics → support.
- Expandable **Trouble with Shizuku / Fold shutting off?** guide immediately below styles, including Windows TCP setup, unauthorized-device recovery, offline testing, battery settings, lock/sleep checks, and reboot expectations.
- Optional Samsung workarounds are distinguished from verified general steps. The guide does not promise permanent connectivity or repeat the incorrect claim that `adb connect` acquires a wake lock.
- Permanent Ko-fi support section. Optional in-app and notification-shade reminders begin no earlier than 24 hours after a completed working transition, with at most one reminder per 24 hours in each location. Users can dismiss or disable reminders. No features require a donation.

## Known limitations / alpha review

- Samsung still briefly powers displays OFF during primary-display remapping. The fades mask that transition; they do not remove it. Rare abrupt flashes remain possible.
- Wallpaper may briefly expose the underlying live wallpaper, particularly around unlock or host recovery.
- Offline TCP is dependent on ADB remaining available and is not a guarantee against OEM process termination. TCP mode can expose debugging on network interfaces; it is not automatically loopback-only.
- New styles and visual layout need device review. Automated tests cannot certify physical screen behavior, touch routing, or OEM frame pacing.
- Install-over requires the same signing identity. This alpha uses the permanent key used for recent alphas; a 1.7.0 APK signed with an older key cannot be updated in place with this key. Do not uninstall just to diagnose an animation issue.

Official 2.0.0 publication is reserved for explicit approval after alpha review.
